# Scientific review of the methodology revision

The revision makes lattice operations the primary object of study and
retains retrieval benchmarks as controls. Its proofs specialize known
FCA/pattern-structure results; the new contribution is an explicit,
auditable experimental methodology and a measured analysis of its limits.

The raw-vector meet is not silently identified with the join of generated
formal concepts. Closed intents and corpus-relative description transport
have their domains stated explicitly. Transport is monotone as F_k G_l,
but no meet/join homomorphism, infomorphism, bond or neural forward-map
identity is asserted. Inclusion applies on the reference universe used to
fit the transport; it can fail on held-out objects.

All 240 full pair meets and all 240 triple meets have empty test extents
under this extension's exact thresholds. Selected joins admit more items, but their
conditional precision usually does not improve over the stronger
constituent. This is a limitation of the tested rank-selection rule, not
a theorem excluding semantic composition by neural representations.
Transport additions are near category prevalence and can lose original
held-out members. Neither a broader extent nor a later block is treated
as evidence of greater abstraction.

GPT-2 source-token codes, external document-summary codes, and historical
support-floor probes remain different protocols. The new token experiment
uses original amplitudes, including Rio/Janeiro and cross-occurrence
New/de and York/de pairs. All snippets use original token traces. The
AI-edited CSV is audited as a separate reading aid: 823 strings differ
from raw decoding, despite complete row-ID coverage.

## Publication assessment

The expanded article is suitable for author review as a transparent arXiv
methods-and-diagnostics preprint, provided its exploratory scope and
negative findings remain explicit. It does not yet support a claim of
validated semantic composition, increasing abstraction with depth, or
causal feed-forward propagation. A successful build and numerical checks
are separate from scientific acceptance.

For a competitive main-track ML submission, the main remaining needs are:

1. Independent contextual-property annotations, with multiple raters or
   another justified validation process; categories alone are too coarse.
2. A predeclared useful interpretability task on a genuinely new corpus,
   with matched ambiguity and lexical-disjoint entity cases.
3. A principled component-selection strategy evaluated prospectively;
   amplitude rank is scale-dependent and often semantically unselective.
4. Matched interventions with a defined behavioral target, reconstruction
   controls and evidence that feature manipulation changes that target.
5. Further checkpoint/seed replication and a much shorter main paper.

The existing caches permit the executed descriptive analyses but do not
supply gold annotations for fine contextual roles or a justified causal
behavioral target. No numerical semantic or causal result was invented
to fill that gap. Original OpenWebText chunks also lack recoverable
original-document independence for population uncertainty estimates.

## Ranked venue fit, checked 2026-10-03

1. CONCEPTS is the closest methodological audience. It now brings together
   the ICFCA/CLA/ICCS communities. Its official scope includes FCA and
   bridges to language models. The 2026 regular track allowed 16 pages
   including references in LNCS format. That edition is over; consult the
   next announced call rather than treating its deadline as still open.
   https://concepts2026.org/call-for-paper/
2. International Journal of Approximate Reasoning is a plausible journal
   direction after strengthening contextual validation. The official
   CONCEPTS 2026 call included an IJAR journal track for substantial
   original contributions. The publisher's current standalone scope page
   was inaccessible (403), so new submission instructions must be checked.
   https://concepts2026.org/call-for-paper/
3. ICLR is an ambitious later target after the evidence and length gaps
   above are resolved. Sparse coding, interpretability and hybrid AI are
   in its official scope. The 2027 paper deadline was September 25, 2026,
   already past at this review date. The current draft is not a compliant
   submission merely because it compiles in the official template.
   https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers

A prestigious pure-mathematics venue would require a substantially new
mathematical result; the present standard adjunction identities are not
that contribution. No acceptance prediction or public submission is made.
