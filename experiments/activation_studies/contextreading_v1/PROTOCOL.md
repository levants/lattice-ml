# Exploratory contextual reading and closure audit

This follow-up starts from original decoded text and saved activations.
It is exploratory, not a preregistered semantic evaluation. Earlier corpus
results and some labels are already known. New GPT-2 reading uses no labels;
external dataset labels are withheld from reading packets and consulted
only afterward. This is not a claim of analyst blinding.

Source occurrences are fixed from the four previously replayed GPT-2 rows.
Every position is zero-based. Full vectors and original-amplitude ranks
1, 2, 3, and alternating ranks 2/3 are compared. Larger groups include
four and six occurrences. Rank ties use increasing coordinate index.
All six SAE/transcoder conditions at blocks 0, 8 and 11 are retained.
No threshold fitting or support-floor replacement is performed.

Groups cover biological specimens, research actions, contextual city/team
roles, assessment language, and deliberately mixed domains. These group
names describe analyst motivations, not asserted feature meanings.

For each group and projection rule, compute all constituent extents,
coordinatewise meet and join, the meet's additional extent, and F(G(q)).
Verify G(F(G(q))) = G(q), join/intersection equality and meet/union inclusion.
The corpus is all 25,600 original 128-token rows, including initial special
tokens, consistently with the legacy cache. Refresh only the four replayed
source rows in memory, exactly as the previous study. All other cached rows
remain unchanged. Membership uses stored amplitudes with no epsilon.

Read all members of small selected cases; otherwise sample the first two
non-source rows plus six uniformly drawn without replacement, seed 20261004.
Selection of cases for deeper reading may be adaptive and is recorded.
Retain universal, empty, nested, and ambiguous outputs in the full records.
Do not turn qualitative readings into independent precision estimates.

Inspect structural explanations, especially special-token contributions,
title punctuation, text splicing, and discourse roles. Full-item themes do
not prove that an earlier source token represents later text. Token replay
is a separate, bounded stage; record inequalities, S/D/R/N and any changed
membership. The corrected CSV is a reading aid, never alignment evidence.
