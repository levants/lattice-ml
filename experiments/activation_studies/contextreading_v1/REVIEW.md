# Scientific review and publication assessment

Review date: 2026-10-03. This continues the lattice-methods revision and
adds text-first analysis; earlier experiments remain in the manuscript.

## What is established

The finite extent identities, Galois connection, description closure and
corpus-induced inclusion are standard order-theoretic consequences under
the stated assumptions. They are not claimed as new mathematics. The new
study explicitly distinguishes raw token operations from closed intents
and from meets of tokenwise-joined item summaries. FG leaves the returned
set unchanged on its fixed corpus. Empty extents use the recorded complete
observed lattice bound; zero queries retrieve the whole corpus.

## Newly executed evidence

The 330 constructions cover related and deliberately mixed groups of up
to six contextual occurrences in six GPT-2 dictionaries. The independent
audit checks 1,686 query records and 157 token-witness records. Nine tables
add original-text examples, extent turnover, leave-one-component-out
counts, controlled input contrasts and structural counterexamples.

The strongest candidate association combines a put/place complement
response with sports-team references. All six joint members support this
reading; targeted edits test seven source/returned contexts. Other verbs
zero the selected activation, whereas within-family alternatives remain
positive but usually fail the original amplitude threshold. This is a
bounded input effect on an observed latent response, not proof that this
latent mediates model behavior. No reconstruction-fidelity result is added.

Research meets admit cross-domain evidence discourse. Larger biological
joins expose redundancy. Cat/dog examples include animal-named fiction as
well as unresolved passages. New/de remains lexically dependent and can
span unrelated spliced texts. Pythia punctuation and SmolLM2 fragment
patterns demonstrate why coarse category mismatch is not equivalent to
absence of a relationship. Their counterexamples remain visible.

## Limitations that remain

- All interpretations and selected follow-ups are exploratory. Known source
  labels and earlier results preclude a claim of analyst blinding.
- No independent contextual gold annotations or new corpus replication
  were added. Label comparison follows text reading but does not validate
  the proposed syntactic or discourse labels.
- Original 128-token web chunks can splice documents and repeat related
  material. Independent-document uncertainty cannot be reconstructed from
  these cached row IDs. Qualitative counts are not semantic precision.
- Exact amplitude queries depend on dictionary scaling and historical
  numerical provenance. Inspected memberships survive replay, but the
  whole population was not re-extracted. BOS-related floor diagnostics
  caution against semantic interpretations of universal closures.
- Cross-block extent turnover is descriptive. No index alignment,
  infomorphism, bond, homomorphism or neural-forward-map claim is added.
- No latent ablation, reconstruction-controlled behavioral experiment,
  or mediated causal-pathway result was executed. Input contrasts are
  narrower evidence and were selected adaptively.
- The full article is long and the ICLR mode is a working draft, not a
  page-budget-compliant submission. More experiments alone do not resolve
  this exposition problem.

## Readiness

An author-reviewed arXiv methods-and-diagnostics release is reasonable once
the author endorses the claims, excerpt presentation and attribution, and
a versioned reproducibility deposit is prepared. The locally verified PDF
and source bundle are not a public submission or data deposit.

The evidence is stronger and more informative than a retrieval-only
presentation, but it is not yet a convincing main-track ML submission.
The next substantive step is prospective testing of the discovered roles:
freeze queries and hypotheses; obtain blinded contextual annotations on
independent document groups; compare exact and relaxed amplitudes with
matched controls; then test latent-mediated effects on a defined task
with reconstruction/error accounting. Shorten the main narrative to that
specific contribution. Do not advertise standard FCA identities as a new
mathematical theorem or assume nonmonotonic extents form a semantic ladder.

## Ranked venue fit, checked against official pages

1. CONCEPTS is the closest audience for lattice/FCA methodology connecting
   conceptual structures with language models. Its 2026 call explicitly
   included this bridge and used a 16-page LNCS regular-paper limit,
   including references. That edition is historical; use the next call.
   https://concepts2026.org/call-for-paper/
2. International Journal of Approximate Reasoning is a plausible expanded
   journal direction. The official CONCEPTS call offered an IJAR journal
   track for substantial original contributions. The current publisher
   scope endpoint returned 403, so standalone submission requirements
   must be verified before choosing that route.
   https://concepts2026.org/call-for-paper/
3. ICLR is an ambitious later target after independent validation and
   compression. Its official scope includes sparse coding, interpretation
   and hybrid AI. The ICLR 2027 deadline was September 25, 2026, already
   past at this review date. An arXiv paper is allowed subject to the
   stated dual-submission policy; an official-template build is not itself
   evidence of submission readiness.
   https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers

A prestigious pure-mathematics journal would need a genuinely new theorem
or structural result; the present contribution is empirical methodology.
No acceptance or future deadline is inferred from a past call.
