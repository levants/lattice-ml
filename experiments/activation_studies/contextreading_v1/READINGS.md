# What the lattice operations select, and why

## Scope and evidential levels

The starting point was the original text, not a category score. All 25
Cat/dog members, 13 New/de members, six virus/Clippers members, and three
biological-join members were read in full. Larger chosen research extents
used a fixed first-two-plus-six sample, seed 20261004. The samples omit the
four source rows. Related passages and visible splices are retained.
The case choices are exploratory, not a blinded evaluation; earlier labels
and results were already known. External labels were withheld from the new
reading packets and consulted after the interpretation ledger was saved.

There are three different kinds of conclusion:

1. **Mathematical:** joins intersect extents; meets contain the constituent
   union; closing a description by FG leaves its extent unchanged.
2. **Numerical:** saved amplitudes, exact inequalities, extent counts and
   token positions certify why a row belongs. They do not explain meaning.
3. **Interpretive:** full passages support possible topical, discourse,
   syntactic or structural relations. Counterexamples narrow these claims.

A raw meet of token codes is not the meet of their pooled source-item
summaries. It need not be a closed concept intent. For each query q we
separately computed FG(q), verified q <= FG(q), and verified GFG(q)=G(q).
Closing q therefore does not discover additional text rows on this fixed
corpus. The additional meet extent E comes from weakening constituent
requirements, not from a mysterious semantic effect of closure.

## An unexpected relation: placement complements and sports teams

Expression: p_virus^(1) join p_Clippers^(1), from 3457@65 and 1924@39,
GPT-2-small/ReLU transcoder block 11. Exact requirements are
4355:11.577988624572754 and 13040:23.46306037902832.

Constituent extents: 537 and 198. Join: six rows. Meet: zero, all 25,600
rows. Retentions: 1.1173% and 3.0303%; both components restrict retrieval.
All six joined rows lack both source words, and all are distributed:

| Row | Coordinate 4355 witness | Coordinate 13040 witness |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5342 | it@108 in "put it together" | Rockies@34 |
| 9155 | Wilson@36 in "put fellow corner Josh Wilson" | Redskins@29/88 |
| 9486 | money@22 in "placing money" | Royals@110 |
| 10441 | them@120 in "puts them" | Seahawks@111 |
| 14210 | brand@55 in "put this brand on players" | Orioles@86 |
| 25220 | points@117 in "put points on the board" | Vikings/Lions@44/67/102 |

The source virus itself follows "studies put the Zika virus". Individual
4355 samples include placing phones on a desk, objects put under stress,
placing buildings alongside others, and putting an institution in a
politically difficult position. Individual 13040 samples span several
sports and team names. The relation is therefore more plausibly a
contextual placement-construction role plus sports references than a
biological property joined with a particular basketball entity.

A targeted input contrast tests this candidate explanation. One earlier
verb is replaced, keeping token count and target fixed. Replacements
introduced/get/used/spending/leaves/apply/score make 4355 exactly zero at
all seven source/returned targets. After seeing that result, alternatives
within the put/place family were tried: all remain positive, but six fall
below the original query amplitude. The saved table lists every value.
Suffix changes leave the complete earlier target vector unchanged.

This supports dependence of this readout on the chosen verb context and
exposes amplitude sensitivity. It does not prove synonym invariance,
a unique latent meaning, a mediated effect on model behavior, or a
feed-forward causal pathway. The family control was adaptive. No surrogate
latent was ablated and no reconstruction-controlled behavioral endpoint
was evaluated.

## Four components do not imply four independent properties

Expression: rank-one joins from monkeys (3457@55), mice (3457@57),
specimens (5411@31), and species (5411@74), transcoder block 11.

| Source | Coordinate | Amplitude | Extent | Extent without it |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| monkeys | 16100 | 5.92418 | 349 | 4 |
| mice | 15788 | 8.90475 | 351 | 35 |
| specimens | 3493 | 6.11186 | 1010 | 3 |
| species | 16566 | 4.35826 | 148 | 66 |

The join returns rows 11388, 23609 and 24255, all distributed. Full texts
concern pituitary/retinal tissue development, mouse longevity and animal
lifespan experiments. Row 23609 visibly continues into political comments.
Several coordinate witnesses concern mice, tissues and biological
structures; the specimens-derived coordinate also has generic fragments,
relative pronouns or punctuation. Removing it leaves the same three rows.
Consequently, individual retention ratios alone would overstate independent
combination. The four distinct one-coordinate vectors have zero meet.

A six-source control joins New/de/Cat/Cavaliers/monkeys/specimens. At
transcoder block 11 every rule (full, ranks 1/2/3, alternating 2/3) has
empty join and universal zero meet. The urban/team group City/Cleveland/
Paulo/city has the same pattern. Nonempty constituents can be incompatible;
no semantic judgment follows from either empty or universal outcomes alone.

## A selective but incompletely explained mixed-domain join

The rank-three dog/lab/rating join at transcoder block 11 uses
12780:10.132925, 6383:5.553518, 4034:6.612775. The constituent counts are
128, 929 and 2315; the join returns only row 12902. Removing successive
components gives 85, 12 and 8 rows, so each contributes a restriction.

The text discusses service-animal rules, a dog on a bus, and institutional
requirements. Numerical witnesses are animal@25, bus@66 and Department@4.
The animal connection is explicit. Spatial and institutional roles are
possible explanations of the other components, consistent with some
constituent samples, but no stable meaning has been established for them.
This is an informative unresolved example, not evidence that lab and
basketball rating compose a service-animal concept.

## Structural relations across coarse category boundaries

For each historical DBpedia join, sixteen members and sixteen nonmembers
were sampled, seed 20261004, without new label selection. The initial
interpretation was saved before the label comparison, not claimed blinded.
Pythia-70M-deduped/TopK20 SAE block 3 residual-post uses one coordinate,
1512:57.30409. SmolLM2-135M/TopK32 SAE block 15 MLP output uses one,
3328:31.25120. Each is a same-coordinate source join, not two features.

All sixteen Pythia members have an early full-stop witness: fourteen at
a title boundary and two in St. abbreviations within the title. Nonmembers
also have title punctuation; its existence alone does not meet the amplitude
requirement. The SmolLM2 sample often highlights names, titles or
romanizations, but row 1810 satisfies the query only at a space and digits
of 2010. A universal name-fragment interpretation fails this counterexample.

Only afterward were categories consulted: Pythia spans nine classes,
SmolLM2 ten; source-category NaturalPlace appears in two and zero members,
respectively. Poor category specificity and structural association can
coexist. These are not independently annotated semantic precision rates.

## What remains to establish

The strongest new evidence concerns contextual verb-family sensitivity,
research discourse across topics, and structural regularities outside the
source category. These relations are supported by exact membership and
close reading, with one bounded input-control study. They remain exploratory
and may be dictionary-, corpus-, or threshold-dependent.

An independent study should freeze these hypotheses, separate document
families, annotate contextual roles without seeing the selected query,
include matched ambiguous and unrelated controls, and evaluate the whole
extent or an independently drawn sample. Causal propagation additionally
requires latent interventions, reconstruction/error controls and a defined
behavioral endpoint. No unseen relationship is asserted solely because a
human reader cannot identify one, and no row is declared irrelevant solely
because its category differs from the source.
